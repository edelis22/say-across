Research notes

A quicker handoff between speakers.

01

Listen

Whisper recognizes the words and selects the direction from the two chosen languages.

02

Translate

NLLB translates the transcript. On a short pause the app can prepare the turn, then hold the output until the pause confirms it.

03

Speak

Captions appear as soon as the translation is ready. The local app uses pitch, pace, and emphasis from the recording for playback.

2.02 s → 1.49 s median to caption across three smooth test turns on one Mac. The test kept the same 0.75-second pause.

A small FLEURS pilot across 11 non-English languages measured lower transcription error with Whisper Turbo. It used clean read speech. Testing conversational accents, noise, and perceived emotional fidelity remains part of the study. FLEURS benchmark
